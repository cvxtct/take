# Responsible use

## Purpose

Take was created while learning musical timing: record a short 10–60 second
passage, listen closely, and study its rhythm and placement within a bar.
The project values the artists behind that learning, particularly independent
and underground musicians. Buy their music, use authorized downloads, and
support them directly when you can.

## Respect the recording and its creator

- Capture audio you own or have permission or another applicable legal basis
  to record. Respect the source platform's terms as well as applicable law.
- Prefer artist-provided downloads and explicitly licensed material for study.
- Do not use Take as a substitute for buying music, to distribute unauthorized
  copies, or to bypass access controls or copy protection.
- Permission to listen does not automatically include permission to record,
  share, or reuse a sample in a released track. Check the rights for your use.
- Neither a 10–60 second duration nor a learning purpose automatically makes
  copying permitted. Private-copying exceptions depend on the jurisdiction and
  circumstances; do not assume they cover sharing or uploading for analysis.

These are project expectations and general guidance, not individualized legal
advice. The MIT license covers Take's software; it grants no rights to music
or other audio captured with it and is not changed by this guidance.

## What Take currently does—and cannot enforce

Take records the selected application's audio through Apple's ScreenCaptureKit.
Browser capture is application-wide: audio may come from multiple tabs.
Take does not inspect browsing history, identify the source website, or enforce
a domain blocklist. **Bandcamp and other websites are not currently blocked.**

A reliable website restriction would require additional browser integration
and a design that handles background tabs, embedded players, and changes during
recording. Checking the foreground tab alone would not identify all captured
audio. A blocklist would still not determine whether a user has permission or
prevent all misuse; this open-source program can also be modified.

Short duration presets support the original learning use case. Timed recording
currently allows up to 60 minutes, and manual recording continues until stopped
or the WAV size limit is reached. Longer recording is a capability, not a
statement about what the user is allowed to record. No duration limit alone
can establish permission or guarantee that copying will not be misused.
